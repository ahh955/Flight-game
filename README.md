A 3D arcade flight game built for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/best-flight-game-20261126-0057) **best-flight-game-20261126-0057** bounty ($500, 50 places). Fly a little coral seaplane off a mountain island's runway, thread 8 glowing rings across a volcanic archipelago — ring 3 hangs right above the glowing crater of Gunung Api, the active volcano — and land back on the runway before the 180-second timer runs out. After the first ring, two enemy fighters jump you — but you are not alone: a wingman in blue-winged coral flies formation on your right and shoots with you, as if at war. Pure vanilla JavaScript — a software 3D renderer written from scratch (no WebGL, no libraries, no network), so the game runs offline from static files in any desktop browser.

## How to play

Open `game/index.html` in a desktop browser.

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| W / S | Throttle up / down |
| ↑ / ↓ | Pitch (stick) |
| ← / → | Roll (bank) |
| A / D | Rudder (yaw) |
| C | Switch camera (chase / cockpit) |
| R | Restart |
| Space / click | FIRE (once the dogfight starts) |
| Enter / click | Start from the menu |

**Goal:** take off from the runway, fly through all 8 gold rings in order, then land back on the runway. Each ring is **+100** points; a smooth landing after all rings adds a **+500** bonus. The timer is 180 seconds — crash into terrain or the sea and the flight ends.

## Dogfight

After the first ring, two enemy fighters (dark charcoal, dark-red fin) chase you and shoot back. Your **wingman** — a second coral plane with blue wings and fin — holds formation on your right and fires at the same enemies; he cannot be shot down. Your bullets do 25 damage (4 hits kill an enemy, **+200** points each), the wingman's do 15, enemy rounds do 8 to your hull. Watch the **HULL** bar in the score panel: at 0 your plane goes down in flames and the mission fails. Enemy fighters keep their distance, break away when your tracers close in, and shoot with roughly one-in-three accuracy, so the rings stay finishable while the sky is a war zone.

**HUD:** compass strip, airspeed and altitude gauges, artificial horizon, throttle bar, score / rings / kills / timer / hull panel, a live minimap of the islands with the next ring marked, and a next-ring hint with distance. The next ring glows gold and pulses; rings you have passed turn green. Enemy HP bars float above damaged fighters.

## The world

Five islands in a tropical sea, dominated by **Gunung Api**, an active volcano in the north-west: a glowing crater with a white-hot heart, three lava flows running down its dark basalt flanks, a smoke column leaning east on the wind, and ash drifting in the air. The other islands share the volcanic mood — black-sand beaches, green slopes, rock cliffs. The custom software renderer paints it all with real light: a sun with direction and glare, diffuse + specular shading on the plane's painted metal, soft shadows under the aircraft and the clouds, layered clouds and smoke with lit tops and shaded bellies, a sea that glitters toward the sun and turns pale turquoise over the sand shelves, and a day cycle (dawn, noon, sunset palettes with a moving sun). The plane itself is drawn from triangles (coral fuselage, cream wings, teal belly, gold tail fin, spinning propeller with a blur disc, panel seams, a canopy that catches the sky). Ring-pass bursts, a landing chime, and a camera that follows the plane in chase or cockpit view.

## Files

- `main.ts` — the entrypoint: copies `game/` and `screenshots/` to `/output` and writes `dossier.json` via the official make.fun vendor library.
- `game/` — the playable game (`index.html` + `src/*.js`, ES modules, no dependencies).
- `screenshots/` — four renders of the real game: takeoff from the runway (cockpit, dawn), threading a ring (chase view), the sunset dogfight in front of Gunung Api (you and your wingman trading fire with two enemy fighters, the volcano's crater and smoke behind them), and the mission-complete landing screen.
- `dossier.json` — generated by `main.ts`; byte-identical to what `buildDossier` writes from `game/` and `screenshots/`.
- `vendor/makefun-game.ts` — the official make.fun game library, copied unchanged.
