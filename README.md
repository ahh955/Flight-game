A 3D arcade flight game built for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/best-flight-game-20261126-0057) **best-flight-game-20261126-0057** bounty ($500, 50 places). Fly a little coral seaplane off a mountain island's runway, thread 8 glowing rings across the archipelago, and land back on the runway before the 180-second timer runs out. After the first ring, two enemy fighters jump you — but you are not alone: a wingman in blue-winged coral flies formation on your right and shoots with you, as if at war. Pure vanilla JavaScript — a software 3D renderer written from scratch (no WebGL, no libraries, no network), so the game runs offline from static files in any desktop browser.

## How to play

Open `game/index.html` in a desktop browser.

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| W / S | Throttle up / down |
| ↑ / ↓ | Pitch (stick) |
| ← / → | Roll (bank) |
| A / D | Rudder (yaw) |
| C | Switch camera (chase / cockpit) |
| R | Restart |
| Space / click | FIRE (once the dogfight starts) |
| Enter / click | Start from the menu |

**Goal:** take off from the runway, fly through all 8 gold rings in order, then land back on the runway. Each ring is **+100** points; a smooth landing after all rings adds a **+500** bonus. The timer is 180 seconds — crash into terrain or the sea and the flight ends.

## Dogfight

After the first ring, two enemy fighters (dark charcoal, dark-red fin) chase you and shoot back. Your **wingman** — a second coral plane with blue wings and fin — holds formation on your right and fires at the same enemies; he cannot be shot down. Your bullets do 25 damage (4 hits kill an enemy, **+200** points each), the wingman's do 15, enemy rounds do 8 to your hull. Watch the **HULL** bar in the score panel: at 0 your plane goes down in flames and the mission fails. Enemy fighters keep their distance, break away when your tracers close in, and shoot with roughly one-in-three accuracy, so the rings stay finishable while the sky is a war zone.

**HUD:** compass strip, airspeed and altitude gauges, artificial horizon, throttle bar, score / rings / kills / timer / hull panel, a live minimap of the islands with the next ring marked, and a next-ring hint with distance. The next ring glows gold and pulses; rings you have passed turn green. Enemy HP bars float above damaged fighters.

## The world

Five islands in a tropical sea, rendered by a custom software renderer: perspective-projected terrain mesh with flat-shaded slopes, beaches, a sea grid, drifting clouds, a day cycle (dawn, noon, sunset palettes with a moving sun), and a low-poly plane drawn from triangles (coral fuselage, cream wings, teal belly, gold tail fin, spinning propeller). Ring-pass bursts, a landing chime, and a camera that follows the plane in chase or cockpit view.

## Files

- `main.ts` — the entrypoint: copies `game/` and `screenshots/` to `/output` and writes `dossier.json` via the official make.fun vendor library.
- `game/` — the playable game (`index.html` + `src/*.js`, ES modules, no dependencies).
- `screenshots/` — four renders of the real game: takeoff from the runway (cockpit, dawn), threading a ring (chase view), the sunset dogfight (you and your wingman trading fire with two enemy fighters), and the mission-complete landing screen.
- `dossier.json` — generated by `main.ts`; byte-identical to what `buildDossier` writes from `game/` and `screenshots/`.
- `vendor/makefun-game.ts` — the official make.fun game library, copied unchanged.
